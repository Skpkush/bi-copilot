# LinkedIn Launch Post

Business analysts spend 60% of their time answering the same questions. I built something to change that.

Most teams keep revenue data in SQL tables and policy answers buried in documents. Getting one trusted answer means waiting on an analyst, stitching sources together, and hoping the numbers match. BI Copilot is a Streamlit app that lets anyone ask in plain English and get a cited, actionable answer in seconds.

What is under the hood:
• A LangGraph supervisor routes each question to governed SQL, document retrieval, or both—then combines the evidence.
• SQL is validated and read-only; RAG answers cite the exact source file, not a vague “according to our docs.”
• The Streamlit UI includes chat with auto-generated Plotly charts, a data explorer, and a safe SQL runner.

The result: time-to-insight drops from hours to seconds while every number stays traceable.
