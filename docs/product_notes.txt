SUMMIT OUTDOOR CO. — PRODUCT LIFECYCLE NOTES
Last updated: October 10, 2026 | Owner: Product Marketing

1. DISCONTINUATIONS
Product ID 17 (AeroLite Running Shoe) is being phased out next quarter. The
current model will stop receiving new purchase orders immediately, and the last
customer order date is November 30, 2026. Remaining inventory will be sold
through at full price until November 15, then at a maximum authorized 15%
clearance discount. No additional markdown may be applied without VP of Sales
approval. Warranty and replacement parts remain available for 24 months.

Other lifecycle changes:
- Product ID 22 (Summit Headlamp) is being replaced by Product ID 34 in Q1 2027.
  Existing stock remains sellable through the end of 2026.
- Product ID 26 (Tundra Parka) is seasonal and will be de-emphasized after the
  winter season; it is not fully discontinued.
- Product ID 12 (PowerCore Kettlebell) had a cost-record error in the source
  system. The corrected cost is in the master product table; no customer action
  is needed.

2. UPCOMING LAUNCHES
- AeroLite 2 Running Shoe (Product ID 31): launch February 2027. Lighter midsole,
  recycled upper, and the same fit last as Product ID 17. Pre-orders open
  January 15, 2027. Target list price is $139.
- TrailForge Pro 28L (Product ID 32): launch March 2027. Replaces the current
  TrailForge Daypack in the premium daypack slot. Target list price is $109.
- Summit Alpine Shell (Product ID 33): launch September 2027. A three-layer
  hardshell positioned above the Summit Shell Jacket. Target list price is $229.
- Summit Headlamp 2 (Product ID 34): launch January 2027. USB-C charging and a
  brighter output; same $44 list price.

3. SALES GUIDANCE
When a customer asks about Product ID 17, explain the phase-out, confirm warranty
coverage, and offer the AeroLite 2 pre-order when available. Do not promise
inventory beyond the published sell-through date. For Northeast footwear
bundles, use the standard bundle price and do not add a shipping discount; freight
is handled separately by the regional policy.

Product launch inventory is allocated by region based on the prior model's
sell-through. Northeast receives the largest initial allocation for AeroLite 2
because it was the top region for Product ID 17. Launch allocations are forecasts,
not commitments, and may change after the November demand review.

4. DATA AND REPORTING NOTES
The product master uses product_id as the join key. Product names can contain
legacy formatting such as trailing spaces, so reports should join on product_id
rather than product_name. Discontinued products remain in the warehouse for
historical reporting and should be filtered by lifecycle_status in the future
product dimension. Until that field is available, use this document as the
authoritative phase-out list.
