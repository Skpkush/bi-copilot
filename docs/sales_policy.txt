SUMMIT OUTDOOR CO. — SALES, DISCOUNT & RETURN POLICY
Effective date: January 1, 2026 | Owner: Revenue Operations | Version 4.2

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy governs list pricing, discounts, approvals, returns, and regional
price execution for all direct and partner-assisted sales in North America. It
applies to orders booked in the CRM and fulfilled from any Summit Outdoor Co.
distribution center. When a customer contract conflicts with this policy, the
signed contract controls, but the deal desk must record the exception.

2. DISCOUNT AUTHORITY
Discounts are calculated on the merchandise subtotal before tax and shipping.

- 0% to 10%: Account Executive may apply without additional approval.
- Above 10% and up to 15%: written Sales Manager approval is required.
- Above 15%: manager approval is required, plus VP of Sales sign-off and a
  documented business case. Discounts above 15% require manager approval.
- Above 25%: CFO approval is required. These deals are reviewed weekly by the
  Revenue Committee and are expected to be rare strategic exceptions.

Orders with a merchandise subtotal above $1,000:
- The standard self-service discount cap is 10%.
- Discounts from 10.1% through 15% require Sales Manager approval entered in
  the CRM before the quote is sent.
- Discounts above 15% require manager and VP of Sales approval, a margin
  analysis, and a defined renewal or volume commitment.
- Orders above $1,000 cannot be split into smaller orders to avoid the approval
  threshold. Revenue Operations audits split-order patterns monthly.

All discounts must be attached to a valid quote, reason code, and approver
identity. Retroactive discounts are not permitted. Discounts may not be stacked
with promotional codes unless the promotion terms explicitly allow it. The
maximum combined discount is 25% absent CFO approval.

3. MARGIN AND PRICE FLOORS
Every quote must clear the product price floor, defined as cost plus a 35% gross
margin. No discount may take a product below its floor without CFO approval.
Shipping surcharges are pass-through charges and must not be absorbed by
increasing the merchandise discount. Sales teams should use the CRM margin
calculator before submitting an exception request.

4. REGIONAL PRICING GUIDELINES
Regional list prices reflect local cost-to-serve, freight lanes, and tax
requirements. The standard regional index is:
- Northeast: +3.0% (higher last-mile and port access costs)
- West: +1.5%
- Southeast: 0.0%
- Midwest: -1.0%
- Southwest: -0.5%

The regional index is applied to the national list price before discounting.
Account Executives may not adjust the index. Requests for a regional exception
must go to the Deal Desk and include a freight quote. Northeast surcharges rose
in Q3 2026 because of constrained carrier capacity; they are reviewed quarterly.

5. RETURN AND EXCHANGE POLICY
Customers may return unused, resalable merchandise within 30 days of delivery
for a full refund of the merchandise price. Returns after 30 days and up to 60
days may be accepted for store credit with manager approval. Items must include
original packaging, tags, and proof of purchase.

- Opened electronics and used fitness equipment are subject to a 15%
  restocking fee.
- Custom or personalized items are final sale unless defective.
- Defective items are replaced at no cost; shipping charges are refunded.
- Return shipping is free for defective goods and for orders above $1,000.
  Otherwise, a $12 return label fee is deducted from the refund.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days
  after the warehouse inspection.

6. ORDER CANCELLATION AND STATUS
Orders may be cancelled without fee before fulfillment. After fulfillment, the
return process applies. Orders in Cancelled or Returned status are excluded from
booked revenue and sales-commission calculations. Pending orders are included
in pipeline reporting but not in recognized revenue.

7. GOVERNANCE AND AUDIT
Sales Managers review discount exceptions weekly. Revenue Operations samples 10%
of approvals each month for completeness. Missing approvals are escalated to the
regional director. Repeated policy violations may result in quote privileges
being revoked. Questions about this policy should go to revenue.ops@summit.example.
