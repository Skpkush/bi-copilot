SUMMIT OUTDOOR CO. — Q3 2026 PERFORMANCE SUMMARY
Prepared by: Commercial Analytics | Published: October 8, 2026
Scope: Orders booked July 1–September 30, 2026; net of cancellations and returns.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Q3 2026 was a strong revenue quarter with a difficult margin story. Net revenue
reached $33.5K, up 20.1% from $27.9K in Q2. Order volume rose across footwear
and camping, and average order value held at $185. Northeast remained our largest
named region at $11.6K, or roughly 35% of net revenue.

Profit margins dropped 8% due to increased shipping costs in the Northeast.
The Northeast carried the highest revenue exposure and the sharpest increase in
cost-to-serve, so it was the region most affected by the margin decline. Carrier
capacity constraints raised last-mile surcharges, while the region's 3% price
index was not enough to offset the increase. The company absorbed most of the
surcharge rather than passing it through mid-quarter.

REVENUE AND DEMAND
- Net revenue: $33.5K, +20.1% quarter over quarter.
- Completed orders: 150. Shipped orders: 24. Cancelled: 8. Returned: 6.
- Northeast: $11.6K net revenue, 46 revenue-bearing orders, the largest named region.
- Southeast: $9.1K. Midwest: $4.2K. Southwest: $3.4K. West: $3.0K.
- Footwear led category growth, with Velocity Trail Shoe and the AeroLite Running
  Shoe (Product ID 17) among the top three products in the Northeast.
- Discounting was broadly stable, but the share of orders above $1,000 increased.

MARGIN AND COST PRESSURE
Gross margin in the Northeast compressed sharply in Q3 as cost-to-serve rose. The
largest drivers were outbound parcel surcharges, a 12% increase in expedited
shipping, and a heavier mix of footwear, which ships in larger cartons. The
shipping increase alone explains roughly six of the eight margin points; the
remaining pressure came from markdowns on discontinued colors.

This is a cost-to-serve issue, not a pricing-power issue. List-price realization
was stable outside authorized discounts. The warehouse does not currently store
carrier invoices or per-order shipping cost, so shipping impact must be analyzed
alongside the finance freight report rather than from order data alone.

CUSTOMER AND PRODUCT NOTES
- Repeat customers generated 61% of net revenue.
- Gold and Platinum loyalty members increased order frequency by 9%.
- The AeroLite Running Shoe (Product ID 17) was among the top three revenue
  products in the Northeast. It is being phased out next quarter as the
  replacement model ramps.
- Product ID 17 is being phased out next quarter. Sell-through of remaining
  inventory is the priority; no new purchase orders will be placed.

RISKS AND Q4 ACTIONS
1. Rebid the Northeast parcel contract before the holiday peak.
2. Add a shipping surcharge review to the weekly margin meeting.
3. Hold discount approvals at current thresholds; do not use discounting to
   offset freight costs.
4. Clear Product ID 17 inventory and prepare the AeroLite 2 launch.
5. Keep a $1,000 order-level margin review for Northeast footwear bundles.

OUTLOOK
Q4 revenue is expected to grow sequentially, but Northeast margin recovery
depends on carrier renegotiation and freight surcharge pass-through. Finance
will report shipping cost per order starting in November.
