1. Q3 revenue was $33,522, +20.1% vs Q2, on 181 orders (+32.1%). AOV moved -9.1%.

3. Footwear is the largest category (33.0% of Q3 revenue) and also has the thinnest margin (53.4%): the biggest revenue line is the least profitable per dollar, so footwear pricing and supplier cost are the highest-leverage margin levers.
4. Orders discounted Over 15% earn 47.5% margin vs 58.8% at full price, with 2.52 vs 2.74 units per order: deep discounts are not buying extra volume.
5. The top 10% of customers (18) generate 29.2% of revenue.
6. 41 high-value customers are 'At Risk' (no recent order), holding 35.2% of lifetime revenue: the first win-back campaign target.
7. On average 44.8% of a monthly cohort orders again within 3 months (cohorts with a full 3-month window).
